FOUNDATION

by Issac Asimov

Trantor is a heavy industrial world, located at the galactic center. The plant is characterized by a dense network of factories, power plants, and refineries that cover the entire surface of the planet. The air is thick with smog and pollution, and the constant hum of machinery fills the atmosphere. The cityscape is dominated by towering skyscrapers and massive industrial complexes, with little to no greenery or natural landscapes visible.
